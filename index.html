About this index

PSI (Pollutant Standards Index) is NEA's 24‑hour measure for Singapore, built from PM2.5, PM10, SO₂, CO, O₃ and NO₂ sub‑indices. ASEAN capitals are shown on the US EPA AQI scale, which uses closely comparable breakpoints.

Severity bands

  • 0–50 Good
  • 51–100 Moderate
  • 101–200 Unhealthy
  • 201–300 Very unhealthy
  • 301+ Hazardous
